# KB Article 001 — Leave Policy
**Organization:** Karan Systems Pvt. Ltd.
**Department:** Human Resources
**Version:** 2.1
**Last Updated:** April 2026
**Access Level:** All Employees

---

## 1. Overview

Karan Systems Pvt. Ltd. is committed to maintaining a healthy work-life balance for all employees. This policy outlines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility criteria, procedures for applying, and guidelines for approval. All employees are expected to follow this policy to ensure smooth business operations and fair treatment across teams.

---

## 2. Types of Leave

### 2.1 Earned Leave (EL) / Paid Time Off (PTO)

- **Entitlement:** 20 paid leave days per calendar year (January–December).
- **Accrual:** Leave accrues at the rate of 1.67 days per month.
- **Carry Forward:** A maximum of 5 unused earned leave days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Any leaves beyond 5 days will lapse at year-end.
- **Encashment:** Earned leave beyond the carry-forward limit may be encashed at the employee's basic daily rate, subject to manager and HR approval.
- **Minimum Duration:** EL can be availed for a minimum of 0.5 days (half-day).

### 2.2 Sick Leave (SL)

- **Entitlement:** 12 sick leave days per calendar year.
- **Carry Forward:** Sick leave is **not** carried forward and lapses at year-end.
- **Documentation:** For sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be submitted within 48 hours of returning to work.
- **Half-Day Sick Leave:** Half-day sick leave is allowed and must be recorded in the HRMS portal.

### 2.3 Casual Leave (CL)

- **Entitlement:** 8 casual leave days per calendar year.
- **Purpose:** Intended for unexpected personal situations, family obligations, or urgent personal matters.
- **Carry Forward:** Not applicable. Casual leave lapses at year-end.
- **Restrictions:** Casual leave cannot be clubbed with earned leave or public holidays without prior manager approval.

### 2.4 Maternity Leave

- **Entitlement:** Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks (182 days) of paid maternity leave as per the Maternity Benefit Act, 1961 (Amended 2017).
- **Eligibility:** The employee must have worked for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding the expected date of delivery.
- **Extended Leave:** Additional unpaid leave of up to 12 weeks may be granted on a case-by-case basis with HR approval.
- **Adoption/Surrogacy:** Commissioning mothers (surrogacy) are entitled to 12 weeks of maternity leave.

### 2.5 Paternity Leave

- **Entitlement:** Male employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid paternity leave.
- **Window:** Must be availed within 3 months of the child's birth or legal adoption.
- **Documentation:** Birth certificate or adoption certificate must be submitted to HR within 30 days.

### 2.6 Bereavement Leave

- **Entitlement:** 3 working days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- **Extended:** 1 additional day for travel if the funeral is in a different city.
- **Documentation:** No documentation is required unless HR requests it for payroll processing.

### 2.7 Public Holidays

- **Entitlement:** Karan Systems observes 12 national and regional public holidays per year.
- **Holiday Calendar:** Published annually on the HRMS portal by January 1st of each year.
- **Optional Holidays:** 2 optional/floating holidays are available per year. Employees choose from a list of regional festivals published by HR.

## 3. Leave Application Process

### 3.1 Standard Application

1. Log in to the **Karan HRMS Portal** at `hrms.karansystems.in`.
2. Navigate to **Leave Management → Apply Leave**.
3. Select the leave type, start date, end date, and add comments/reason.
4. Click **Submit for Approval**.
5. The direct reporting manager receives an email notification for approval.
6. The employee receives email confirmation of approval or rejection within 2 business days.

### 3.2 Emergency Leave

- In case of an unforeseen emergency, employees must notify their **direct manager via phone or WhatsApp within 4 hours** of their shift start time.
- The leave application on the HRMS portal must still be filled within **24 hours of return**.
- Failure to inform may result in the absence being marked as Loss of Pay (LOP).

### 3.3 Pre-Planned Leave

- For planned leaves exceeding 3 days, a minimum of **5 business days' advance notice** is required.
- For planned leaves exceeding 10 days, a minimum of **15 business days' advance notice** is required.
- Ensure proper handover documentation is shared with the team and the manager before proceeding on leave.

## 4. Leave Approval Guidelines

| Leave Type      | Approval Authority           | SLA           |
|-----------------|------------------------------|---------------|
| Earned Leave    | Direct Manager               | 2 business days |
| Sick Leave      | Direct Manager (auto-approved for ≤ 3 days) | Immediate |
| Casual Leave    | Direct Manager               | 1 business day |
| Maternity/Paternity | HR + Manager           | 5 business days |
| Bereavement     | Manager (auto-approved)      | Immediate |

---

## 5. Leave Restrictions and Blackout Periods

- Leaves are generally **not approved** during company-defined blackout periods such as quarter-end (last 5 business days of each quarter), major product launches, or critical client deliverable periods.
- Blackout periods are communicated at least 30 days in advance by department heads.
- Emergency situations (medical emergencies, bereavement) are exempt from blackout restrictions.

---

## 6. Negative Leave Balance

- Employees who have exhausted their leave balance may request an advance on their upcoming earned leave up to a maximum of 5 days, subject to manager and HR approval.
- Negative leave balance at the time of resignation will be deducted from the final settlement.
